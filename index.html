Read the result responsibly

What the dashboard can and cannot tell you.

The sliders let you test “what if” assumptions. The model combines a recent observed temperature trend with small, explicitly assumed scenario adjustments. It does not calculate atmospheric chemistry, regional rainfall, extreme-weather probabilities, ice-sheet dynamics, or full ocean circulation. A two-year forecast is especially sensitive to natural variability such as El Niño and La Niña.
